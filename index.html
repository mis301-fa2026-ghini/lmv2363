Your next side quest starts here.

We’re a neighborhood coffee shop in Juniper Falls where a quick coffee can turn into an afternoon around the game table. Come for a drink, something to eat, or a chance to connect.

Our shelves hold physical board games, cards, dice, and chess sets. Whether you play every week or haven’t played in years, there’s room for you at the table.
